# From: Meera Iyer, Head of Compliance — Northwind Life (client)
**Subject: Conditions for any automated change on Northwind workflows**

We're comfortable with Formi experimenting on our renewal and KYC workflows, on these conditions:

1. **No customer-facing change without a named human approval on our side.** That covers anything a
   customer hears: the opening line, the voice, the script. We understand that operational settings such as
   call timing and caller-ID rotation are covered by the policy we signed. If you want to use that latitude,
   tell us in the weekly summary what changed and why.
2. **Script changes need validation evidence** attached to the approval request. "The model thinks it's
   better" isn't evidence.
3. **Our IRDAI auditors may ask why any given customer heard what they heard on a given day.** We need that
   answer within 24 hours, down to the variant and the reason it was live.
4. We'd rather you were slow and right. A change that makes things worse for a cohort of our customers, even
   briefly, is a conversation with our board, not a rounding error.
5. Our approvers work 10:00–19:00 (KYC: 10:00–18:00). Requests sent at night sit in a queue, and our
   approvers are not impressed by a queue.
