# From: Tara Kulkarni, Head of Product
**Subject: Product definitions for the outcome-first loop**

Definitions I need you to hold to:

- **Pickup and engagement are separate KPIs.** Engagement = of the people who pick up, the share who reach a
  conclusion (interested or not). Don't blend them into one score, in the product or in a deck.
- **A cohort is one shared object.** Detection, experiments and the supervisor view all refer to the same
  cohort with the same ID. I don't want three subtly different definitions of "seniors in Tamil Nadu".
- **Minimum cohort size and evaluation window are open questions.** I don't know the right answer. Pick one,
  show me the reasoning, and tell me what it costs (speed vs. false winners).
- **Proposals are a product surface.** Every proposal carries an impact score, a diff, an affected-lead count
  and a dry-run result. We'll rank the supervisor's queue by impact score, so it must mean something.
- **An expired proposal is a rejection.** Re-queue it with fresh data. Don't resubmit the stale one.
- **Only what actually happened counts as evidence.** If an experiment arm never ran because permission
  wasn't given, it must not show up in learning as if it had.
- A winning variant updates that workflow's config only. Promoting it to other clients is a later problem.

Wishlist, all of which I consider P0: per-person call-time (it's the headline for the summit), the
consolidated supervisor view, and caller-ID health. I know that's a lot.
