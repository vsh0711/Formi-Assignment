# From: Aryamann, CTO
**Subject: How I'll read your submission**

The others have told you what they want. Here's how to think about it.

- **We sell outcomes in regulated industries.** An improvement nobody can explain is worth less than a smaller
  one a compliance head can sign off on. An improvement that hurts a cohort is worth less than nothing.
- **The best compute is no compute.** Credits are cheap here and expensive in production. Spend them where
  they change a decision.
- **Scale is the real problem.** Taking one workflow live is never the hard part. Running hundreds without an
  engineer in the loop is. Design for the envelope in the README, and tell me honestly what breaks first.
- **Choosing is the job.** Everyone's P0 can't be P0. Make the call, write down who you said no to, and draft
  the message you'd send them. "I did a bit of everything" is the weakest possible answer.
- **Assume you own this in production next month.** Write the docs for the engineer who gets paged when you're
  on leave.
- Some of what's in the other memos isn't true, or doesn't match the policies the simulator enforces. Trust
  the system's contract over anyone's recollection, including mine.
